# RESPONSIBLE USE

*LadybugBets responsible-use policy.*

This is a short, serious statement of posture. It is not, and does not attempt
to be, a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## What LadybugBets is

LadybugBets is an **analytical and educational** product. It helps people
inspect and understand betting-market prices. It does not accept, transmit, or
settle wagers, and it does not custody funds.

## Risk and uncertainty

- Gambling involves financial risk. Money placed at risk can be lost.
- Market information does not guarantee outcomes.
- Past performance does not establish future results.
- Any probability, movement, or comparison shown by LadybugBets is a derived
  interpretation under stated assumptions, not a certainty.

## User responsibilities

- Users should comply with the laws and regulations applicable to their own
  jurisdiction, including any age and licensing requirements.
- Users are responsible for their own decisions. A Spot is evidence to examine,
  not advice to act.

## Product design commitments

LadybugBets product design will:

- not deliberately target minors, and not use language or imagery aimed at
  minors;
- avoid mechanics that encourage loss-chasing;
- avoid intentionally deceptive urgency mechanics;
- make no claim of guaranteed winning, guaranteed edge, or the ability to beat
  the bookmaker;
- state uncertainty rather than imply certainty.

## Compliance boundary

This document does not replace legal review. **Jurisdiction-specific compliance
must be reviewed before any regulated commercial functionality is introduced.**
The current repository is foundational documentation and does not constitute a
regulated service.
